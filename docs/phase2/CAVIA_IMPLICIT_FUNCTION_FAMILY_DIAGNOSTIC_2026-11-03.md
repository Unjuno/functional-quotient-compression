# CAVIA and implicit-function Mirror family diagnostic — 2026-11-03

## Trigger

The MA-622 → MA-628 sequence produced repeated failures for the same structural reason: a compact Mirror coordinate reproduced the function family, but its strongest ordinary control used the same coordinates/equations and exactly matched quality and payload. By MA-627 and MA-628, the native control was a direct parameterization alias; MA-626 likewise reduced to standard geometric conditioning. MA-628's entropy-coded bitstream also exactly matched ordinary COIN++-style modulation codes.

Under the worker stop rule, pause remaining candidates in this family until the proposal distinguishes a Mirror-specific mechanism from generic latent/modulation coding. This is a family pause, not a conclusion that all neural implicit function families are uncompressible.

## Evidence

- **MA-622 — FAIL, synthetic CAVIA factor context.** Aligned held-out context nRMSE 0; 320 B task state versus 1,024 B independent context. Native direct-angle CAVIA exactly matched outputs, bytes and state. Fresh sealed.
- **MA-623 — FAIL, private CAVIA residual.** In the rank-2 private interaction world, rank 2 missed quality (nRMSE 0.099–0.109), rank 4 passed on one seed only (0.044 vs 0.079), and rank 8 reached zero error but used 4,527–4,529 B versus 3,039–3,045 B independent CAVIA. Native PCA residual exactly aliased Mirror. Fresh sealed.
- **MA-624 — FAIL, examples to code.** Direct CAVIA and pooled context reached numerical-zero held-out error at 1,771–1,783 B and compute proxy 1,024; inferred Mirror code used 2,540 B and proxy 32,768. Fresh sealed.
- **MA-626 — scoped FAIL, canonical circle SDF.** Shared analytic representation used 1,236 B versus 236.7 KB dense grids (~0.52%), but direct native center/radius conditioning exactly matched output and bytes with lower compute. Fresh sealed.
- **MA-627 — FAIL, periodic modulation.** 879–883 B code versus 24.8 KB independent curves, but native modulated activation exactly matched bytes/output/compute. Fresh sealed.
- **MA-628 — FAIL, periodic codec.** At 12 bits, 218–219 B payload reached mean nRMSE 0.00038–0.00039 versus 4,962–5,272 B independent curves. Native COIN++ modulation used the same bitstream and matched every rate-distortion point. Fresh sealed.

All measurements are synthetic mechanism screens; none trained a DeepSDF, SIREN or COIN++ decoder. They do not rule out advantages on natural shape/signal datasets or trained decoders.

## Structural diagnosis

The tested Mirror additions mostly restated known conditioners: CAVIA context vectors, factorized angles, direct center/radius geometry, periodic activation modulation, and quantized modulation vectors. Where sharing compressed storage against independent curves or grids, the same compression came from the established shared decoder plus code. Additional Mirror compute sometimes worsened the frontier.

The next experiment in this family should require a concrete degree of freedom that generic latent adaptation, FiLM, direct coordinate conditioning, activation modulation, and ordinary entropy-coded codes cannot express at equal cost. Until such a proposal is specified, MA-625, MA-629, and MA-630 remain UNTESTED and operationally paused. Continue the global registry queue with an independent P0 family.
