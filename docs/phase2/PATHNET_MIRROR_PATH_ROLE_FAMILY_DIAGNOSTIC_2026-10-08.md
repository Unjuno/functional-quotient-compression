# PathNet path × role Mirror family diagnostic — 2026-10-08

## Decision

Pause unchanged PathNet path-plus-Givens role-view candidates MA-451/452 after two sequential P0 development screens found the same structural cause: their task-specific or factorized Mirror coordinates are exactly ordinary native Givens conditioning. Reopen only with a non-aliased hypothesis or a genuinely different learned/optimized path composition. MA-453 Routing Networks is a distinct dynamic-routing candidate and may proceed with its own native router/block controls; this pause does not establish a result for it.

## Evidence

| MA | Mechanism | Result | Strong control | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 451 | Two-angle role View after a PathNet two-layer route | RMSE 0.3926/0.2776 versus path-only 0.7919/0.6003; 1,557 bytes versus 1,231 path-only; 3.3–5.8× rank-2 adaptation time | Direct native Givens has identical task codes, payload hash and output RMSE | FAIL; aligned role effect, byte/runtime gates missed, no Mirror-specific value |
| 452 | Learned role codebook factorized from path; four of twelve path-role pairs withheld | Held-out RMSE 0.2532/0.2680 versus path-only 1.0623/1.2528; 1,766 bytes versus 1,468 path-only and 1,234 independent-fit vectors | Direct native Givens has identical codebook payload hash and output RMSE; independent fits give RMSE 0.0226/0.0318 | FAIL; compositional generalization is observed, but bytes miss and the mapping is a native role conditioner |

Both screens used small synthetic linear tasks with targets expressly generated from Givens rotations. They establish aligned mechanism behavior only. Neither is a PathNet/Routing Network reproduction or evidence of natural task transfer. Fresh seeds remained sealed after the frozen development gates failed.

## Shared cause and next design rule

The role address is useful for distinguishing functions, including at withheld combinations, but the Givens map is already a direct native functional parameterization. A Mirror claim therefore adds no distinct function in these experiments. Future PathNet work must test a different coordinate family, a learned/optimized router interaction that cannot be expressed by the same native role code, or a deployment frontier that beats exact Givens and simple role-vector controls on actual bytes and runtime.
