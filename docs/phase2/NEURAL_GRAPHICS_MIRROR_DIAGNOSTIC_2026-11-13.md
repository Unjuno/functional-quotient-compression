# Neural graphics scene/object Mirror diagnostic — 2026-11-13

## Decision

Pause the direct scene×time / canonical-object SE(3) coordinate subfamily at MA-904 pending a functional mechanism that differs from native factorization or ordinary instancing. Keep MA-904 UNTESTED; no MA-904 result is inferred from neighboring synthetic screens.

## Facts

- MA-902 synthetic scene×time multiplicative codes saved about 86% actual NPZ bytes versus independent dense full-cell fields, but missed the frozen heldout nRMSE gate in two of three amended fresh seeds. Its output, payload, and compute exactly matched native rank-4 tensor factorization.
- MA-903 synthetic canonical rigid geometry plus primitive SE(3) codes saved about 96.98% actual bytes versus materialized posed clouds with exact heldout composition. After correcting an unfair first control, native SE(3) action parameters byte/output/compute-alias the Mirror representation; transform evaluation adds compute versus cached posed clouds.
- Neither run evaluated real scenes, rendering, occlusion, learned action-conditioned dynamics, or MRO-GWM/C-NGP/K-Planes.

## Interpretation

These two separate mechanism screens establish that naming a scene/time or object/action factor as a Mirror coordinate has no demonstrated advantage when the function is already representable by native tensor factorization or ordinary SE(3) instancing. MA-902 also found a quality/private-function boundary in its synthetic generator. The evidence is sufficient to pause direct coordinate relabelings, not the broader neural graphics family.

## Restart condition for MA-904

Resume with a structural difference such as a learned, geometry-equivariant action-conditioned shared decoder, a cross-object compositional generalization hypothesis beyond bilinear object/action embeddings, or a validated shared/private residual boundary. Require native bilinear/SE(3), simple embedding, independent-object, and action-conditioned controls with real rollout/geometry quality when the official source/data/runtime permit.
