- Do not state or imply endorsement by the University of Minnesota or GroupLens.

- Do not redistribute the data without separate permission.
- Do not use it for commercial or revenue-bearing purposes without prior permission from GroupLens.

The archive remains external to Git. The experiment is a research-only explicit-rating screen, not a commercial recommendation system.
