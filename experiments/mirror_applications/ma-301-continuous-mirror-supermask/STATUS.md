# MA-301 status

**FAIL — amended fresh screen failed quality in two of three worlds.**

## H / T / D / C / U

- **H:** Thirty-two task functions generated by a shared fixed random-feature backbone and task-specific Givens views can be represented by a shared readout plus one low-description Mirror coordinate per task with fewer serialized bytes than binary masks and near-independent quality.
- **T:** Fixed 16-to-32 ReLU random feature encoder; 32 tasks; 600 Adam updates, batch 128. Initial 4-task Torch archive screen failed its storage-access gate and remained development-only. Amended protocol uses compact binary payloads, int8 task angles, development worlds 30110/30111, fresh worlds 30112/30113/30114, LR .003 locked before fresh. Controls: hard tie, packed binary supermask, fp16 continuous gates, Mirror angles, independent readouts.
- **D:** FAIL. Amended Mirror payload was 334 B vs 424 B for packed binary masks (0.788x) and 4,266 B independent. Fresh normalized MSE: 2.14e-5, 0.412, 0.256 for Mirror; independent was 2.79e-4, 2.89e-4, 1.47e-4. Only 1/3 fresh worlds met the independent-quality criterion. Fresh results were replayed exactly; tests 3/3 passed.
- **C:** The failure may be optimization and angle-aliasing under a fixed 600-update budget; the target family is generated by the same Givens coordinate family, but fresh worlds 30113/30114 did not recover it.
- **U:** Near-convergence capacity, learned task inference, natural task masks, continual learning, and direct per-query Mirror runtime remain untested. The reported throughput is only for precomputed effective vectors and excludes coordinate construction.

## Fact / Interpretation / Hypothesis

- **Fact:** The amended Mirror payload is 21.2% smaller than packed binary masks. In two of three fresh worlds it missed independent-readout quality by a large margin. The final deterministic replay reproduced all 35 result rows with zero metric difference and exact payload lengths.
- **Interpretation:** One int8 scalar per task is a real storage reduction for this 32-task aligned construction, but fixed-budget learning did not reliably recover the represented task family.
- **Hypothesis:** Longer or better-conditioned optimization could reduce the fresh failures; this requires a new preregistered screen, not tuning on these fresh results.

The initial 4-task Torch serialization screen and its storage-gate miss are preserved in `protocol_variants/pre_amendment_4task_torch_payload/`. Its results are not pooled with the amended 32-task experiment.
