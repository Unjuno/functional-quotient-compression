# MA-411 — canonical transform with sparse Mirror refinement

## H — falsifiable hypothesis

On context-specific transforms generated by a fixed Hadamard basis plus a small shared-support sparse residual, sparse Mirror coordinates will recover at least 90% of the dense residual's held-out reconstruction quality with less than 40% of its actual payload bytes while keeping every transform's condition number at or below 2.

## T — protocol

Aligned synthetic linear-operator reconstruction with four contexts. Compare canonical-only Hadamard, sparse Mirror coefficients with and without a condition-number constraint, rank-2 context residuals, dense residuals, and independent full matrices. Two development worlds select LR; three fresh worlds are locked. Count actual serialized tensors and sparse support indices. Report held-out normalized RMSE, per-context condition numbers, MAC proxy, and wall time.

This is aligned feasibility evidence. Fresh worlds change residual values but use the registered shared sparse support. It does not imply natural transform variations are sparse.
