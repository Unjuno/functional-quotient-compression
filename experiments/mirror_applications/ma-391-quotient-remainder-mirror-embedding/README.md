# MA-391 — Quotient-remainder compositional embedding Mirror address

Status: **FAIL at the frozen development embedding-fidelity gate; fresh stayed sealed.** Dedicated branch: `research/ma-391-quotient-remainder-mirror-embedding-20261009`.

## Mirror insertion

**Mirror insertion:** add one token-specific Givens angle over the two fixed component rows selected by exact quotient/remainder addresses. The candidate changes the composition coordinate; it does not change the address, source tables or decoder.

## H — Hypothesis

On a deliberately aligned collision-free quotient/remainder embedding fixture, one angle per token can retain vector/task quality and reduce actual complete payload bytes relative to independent embeddings and direct two-coefficient composition, while improving over fixed addition, elementwise product and concatenation-projection.

## T — Frozen design

PA59 supplies the quotient/remainder complementary-partition prior art. Vocabulary size is 1,024 with unique 32×32 quotient/remainder pairs, 16-dimensional fixed component tables, and two development worlds. Compare independent embeddings, fixed addition, elementwise product, concatenation with a paid fixed projection, unconstrained direct coefficients, and Mirror angles. All serialized tables, codes, decoder, projection state and metadata are charged. The complete frozen protocol is `PROTOCOL.json`.

The teacher is intentionally generated by the Mirror orbit. This is a representation-feasibility screen, not a natural-data result. The shared source tables stay fixed to isolate composition/code effects.

## D — Result

**Fact.** Across development seeds 39101/39102, all 1,024 quotient/remainder addresses were unique. Full-table embedding NRMSE was .0247/.0275; direct coefficients reached .00943/.01505; Mirror reached .12475/.13911. Mirror decoder NLL was 2.4858/2.4847 versus direct coefficients 2.4821/2.4803, and top-1 was 99.12%/98.83% versus 99.71%/99.80%. Thus task outputs were close, but the frozen primary embedding-fidelity limit of .03 failed in both worlds. Fixed addition, product and concatenation-projection NRMSE were respectively 1.70/1.73, 1.06/1.05 and 1.47/1.38. Actual Mirror payload was 10,461B in each world: 16.5% of full table payload and 73.6% of direct-coefficient payload. All twelve serialized payloads replayed byte counts, hashes and metrics exactly; four tests pass. Fresh 39111–39113 remained sealed.

**Interpretation.** A single angle is far more useful than fixed composition operators on this aligned fixture and is compact relative to a full embedding table. Its task-classifier outputs are close to generic coefficients despite a large vector-reconstruction error. But direct free coefficients fit the vector target better; this experiment does not establish a Mirror-specific advantage, and the strict preregistered quality gate failed. Mirror also performs the two shared-row lookups and two coefficient-weighted vector operations, plus two trigonometric reconstruction operations per token.

**Hypothesis.** The learned angle optimization under the fixed 2,000-update schedule did not reach the unit-circle teacher as closely as unconstrained coefficients; classifier insensitivity to much of the embedding residual explains the high top-1 despite the NRMSE miss. Longer schedules or alternative angle optimizers were not tested and cannot be inferred from this fixed-budget screen.

## H / T / D / C / U

- **H:** One quotient/remainder token angle preserves embedding/task quality, improves on fixed composition, and compresses the direct two-coefficient representation.
- **T:** PA59; V=1,024, exact collision-free 32×32 addresses, fixed 32×16 component tables, six controls, seeds 39101/39102, Adam 2,000 updates for trainable methods, actual compressed NPZ inference payloads.
- **D:** **FAIL.** Mirror missed the frozen .03 NRMSE threshold in both worlds (.1248/.1391), despite compact storage and near-matched decoder NLL/top-1; fresh remained sealed.
- **C:** Direct coefficients use twice the token-code values but fit the same target more accurately at this update budget. The fixed classifier is insensitive to residual directions, so high accuracy is not proof of embedding fidelity.
- **U:** Natural/recommender vocabularies, learned component tables, longer-convergence optimization, quantized codes, and larger address scaling.
