# MA-272 result reconciliation

## Fact

Two dedicated branches used distinct MA-272 protocols:

- `research/ma-272-oftv2-mirror-input-views-20261008` trained a 16D map on three fresh worlds. It reported 3,272B Mirror versus 7,175B dense OFTv2 and 6,033B independent, with near-zero Mirror MSE. Eager CPU throughput was 0.77–0.83M examples/s, below OFTv2 at 1.16–1.24M.
- `research/ma-272-oftv2-mirror-views-20261008` audited a 16D shared-skew generator on corrected fresh seeds 157/263/359/461. It preserves earlier invalidated accounting versions but excludes them from the final rows. In the final result, shared Mirror and ordinary scalar-times-shared-skew control are identical in function and both cost 3,786B. Exact input-side Mirror transform time averaged 0.335ms, versus 0.138ms for materialized QW. Neumann K=8 error was small (about 5e-9), but its runtime was higher still.

Both raw result sets and verifications are retained; their seeds and protocols are not pooled.

## Disposition

MA-272 is **FAIL for Mirror-specific/runtime Pareto value**. The first screen supports an aligned storage/quality result against dense OFTv2, but it is slower and does not control for scalar generation. The corrected screen shows the scalar generator exactly reproduces Mirror at equal bytes, while input-side execution misses the preregistered latency gate.
