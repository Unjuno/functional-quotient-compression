# MA-725 — Packed-Ensemble subnetworks with Mirror member views

Status: SCREENING

## Hypothesis

H: Four logical members generated by per-member Givens views of one shared hidden backbone retain teacher function quality and ensemble diversity with less serialized state than packed independent subnetworks, and outperform shared-backbone FiLM/BatchEnsemble-style controls at matched bytes.

## Mirror insertion

> **Mirror insertion:** this experiment adds `m`, a per-member bank of hidden-channel Givens angles, to one shared two-layer network so multiple member functions can be recovered without storing a separate backbone per member.

Prior art: Packed-Ensembles PA184, MIMO PA42, BatchEnsemble PA17. Controls: independent members, packed independent members (same weights, batched execution), shared backbone plus task-specific FiLM gains (native control), shared backbone plus Mirror Givens coordinates.

## Frozen protocol

Four synthetic teacher members share a 16-32-1 tanh MLP and differ by 16 paired hidden-channel rotations. D=16, H=32, 4 members, 512 train and 512 held-out examples/member; dev seed 72501. Train student models by MSE for 400 Adam steps, fixed learning rate 1e-2 and batch size 128. Report member MSE, ensemble mean MSE, prediction correlation/diversity, full serialized model-bank bytes, active MAC proxy and CPU inference wall time. Independent and packed controls use identical independent weights; packed only changes batch layout. Shared-FiLM stores one shared backbone plus 32 gains/member. Mirror stores one backbone plus 16 angles/member.

### Gates

PASS requires per-member held-out MSE <=.02, ensemble MSE <=.01, diversity correlation within .05 of independent, >=25% payload reduction vs packed independent, and better quality/byte frontier than FiLM. Native control equivalence or quality/compute miss is FAIL. No audit after frozen development failure.

## Scope

Aligned synthetic teacher functions only. No classification benchmark, natural ensemble, GPU throughput or general ensemble-capacity claim.

## Amendment 01 — batch-size correction and locked fresh replication

The original implementation accidentally used all 512 examples/member per update while the preregistered batch size was 128. Original `RESULTS_CORE.csv` and `RESULTS_FRESH.csv` remain preserved and are not treated as valid confirmation. Before accessing new seeds, amendment 01 froze the correction to minibatch 128 and seeds 72521/72522/72523; architecture, optimizer, 400 updates and gates stayed fixed.

**FACT (amended dev + fresh):** On corrected dev seed 72501, Mirror Givens has mean member MSE .00498, ensemble MSE .00128, payload 5,017 B. Across three fresh seeds (12 member rows), its mean member MSE is .001423, worst member .002104, mean ensemble MSE .000360 and mean pair correlation -.00449. Every fresh member MSE is below .02 and ensemble MSE below .01; mean pair correlation is within .05 of packed independent. Payload is 5,017 B, 56.1% below packed independent (11,421 B) and 4.9% below shared FiLM (5,273 B). FiLM mean fresh member MSE is .01094 (worst .02498), ensemble MSE .00282. CPU inference wall time is .509 ms for Givens vs .268 ms FiLM and .328 ms packed independent, roughly 1.90x and 1.55x slower. Active MAC proxy is 4,544 vs 4,480 FiLM and 4,352 packed. This is a repeated storage/quality improvement with a runtime regression.

**INTERPRETATION:** The aligned teacher uses one common base and Givens views, so this is a favorable mechanism test, not evidence for unrelated independently trained ensembles. The nearest simple FiLM control uses 32 gains/member; Givens uses 16 angles and improves quality while reducing bytes slightly, but its unfused CPU inference is slower. The result improves storage/quality frontier, not compute frontier.

**HYPOTHESIS:** Givens views may be useful when member functions share an aligned hidden orbit and runtime kernels can fuse pair rotations. Neither unrelated-member capacity nor fused implementation is established.

**D:** PROMISING for this aligned synthetic storage/quality screen; not adopted. Fresh replication is 3/3; compute Pareto regresses and the original protocol deviation is preserved.

**C:** The teacher is constructed from a shared backbone plus Givens views, favoring the Mirror representation. The FiLM, packed implementations and eager CPU timing are small synthetic controls, not production kernels.

**U:** Natural classification, independently trained members, GPU/fused kernel runtime, scale-up, accuracy/NLL ensembles, and generalization beyond the teacher orbit.

### H / T / D / C / U

- **H:** Small layer/member-specific Mirror views recover useful logical ensemble members from one physical backbone at lower bytes than packed independent members and simple FiLM.
- **T:** Four 16-32-1 tanh members; 512 train/512 held-out each; 400 Adam updates, minibatch128 (amendment 01), dev 72501 and fresh 72521/22/23; independent, packed, FiLM and Givens controls.
- **D:** PROMISING, 3/3 fresh worlds for storage/quality gates; Givens uses 56.1% fewer bytes than packed and 4.9% fewer than FiLM, but ~1.90x FiLM CPU latency.
- **C:** Strong teacher-family alignment and slow unfused Givens CPU path.
- **U:** Natural-task usefulness, independent functions, optimized runtime, and capacity at near convergence.
