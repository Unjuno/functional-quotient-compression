# MA-416 — shared decoder with per-function Mirror codes

## H — falsifiable hypothesis

A shared decoder plus two per-function Givens coordinates reconstructs a family of continuous functions at nearly the quality of a two-dimensional ordinary latent code, with lower actual payload bytes due to not expanding the decoder input.

## T — protocol

Fit 16 synthetic continuous Fourier functions per world from sampled query points, evaluate on held-out query coordinates, and compare shared-only, two-angle Mirror hidden views, ordinary latent codes of dimensions 1/2/8 concatenated to decoder input, and independent per-function decoders. Development worlds select LR; three fresh worlds are locked. Charge decoder, all function codes, and independent models in actual serialized bytes.

This is a small neural-function reconstruction screen, not DeepSDF or real 3D reconstruction evidence.
