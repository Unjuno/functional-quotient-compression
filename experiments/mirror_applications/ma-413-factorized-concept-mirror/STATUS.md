# MA-413 status: FAIL (strict preregistered Mirror-specific gate)

## H — falsifiable hypothesis

Two attribute-specific Givens codes would compose on a shared MLP and improve held-out 11 accuracy by at least 5pp over factorized FiLM and the direct table, retaining >=90% seen accuracy at <=1.1× FiLM bytes.

## T — executed

Two binary attributes, four combinations; trained non-oracle models on 00/10/01 and held out all 11 training examples. Teacher is explicitly composed from two disjoint input Givens rotations before a shared random MLP. Compared shared, factorized Mirror, factorized FiLM, additive rank-one residual, direct per-combination angle table with 11 code left untrained at zero, and independent MLPs trained on all four combinations (oracle). 300 AdamW updates × batch 128; 2 development worlds × 3 seeds selected LR 0.01 for factor methods and 0.003 for oracle; 3 fresh worlds × 3 seeds. Actual serialized state retained.

## D — FAIL

Fresh mean held-out 11 accuracy: Mirror 70.72%, FiLM 68.45%, direct table 69.97%; Mirror's best margin is +2.27pp vs FiLM and +0.75pp vs direct table, below the required +5pp. Mirror seen accuracy is 70.70%; payload is 10,649B vs FiLM 11,605B (byte gate passes). Shared scored 70.07% held-out; rank-one 68.90%; oracle independent 65.54% (underfit despite seeing held-out training examples). Mirror misses the predeclared quality margin, so no Mirror-specific factorization claim is made.

## C — strongest counter-hypothesis

The teacher was generated by exactly the compositional Givens mechanism being tested, yet its held-out advantage over a direct code table was only 0.75pp. A simple factorized FiLM also came within 2.27pp at slightly more bytes. Thus the result may reflect the ease of this small synthetic classification task and optimizer variability rather than a useful Mirror-specific extrapolation gain.

## U — unresolved

Natural semantic attributes, more factors, noisy/compositional language tasks, near-convergence, and interaction terms remain untested. The oracle control underfit and is not a meaningful capacity upper bound.
