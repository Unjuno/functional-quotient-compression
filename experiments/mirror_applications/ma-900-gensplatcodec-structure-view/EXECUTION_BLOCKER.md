# MA-900 execution blocker

## H — hypothesis

A compact structure-factor × appearance View code can share one geometry-guided one-step Gaussian decoder across logical scene variants and preserve multi-view consistency with a better actual-stream rate-quality-latency frontier than native independent codec state and ordinary latent/gain controls.

## T — review and required controls

Reviewed PA265 and arXiv 2607.24403 metadata/full abstract, searched official-code candidates, checked local packages, devices, CUDA, and scene assets. A valid test needs native GenSplatCodec structure/appearance streams and one-step decoder, a byte-near ordinary factor/latent or photometric control, Mirror factor code, and independent scene state where practical; fresh scenes and camera views must be held out. Charge decoder/checkpoint, structure and appearance streams, codes, quantizers, metadata, and reconstruction state. Match the one-step NFE and report actual coded stream bytes, PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS and cross-view consistency, inference latency and peak memory.

## D — NOT RUN / UNTESTED

No official implementation was located by bounded exact-title search. Current runtime is CPU-only with no CUDA, diffusion package, Gaussian rasterizer, or local benchmark scenes. A different Gaussian codec or toy decoder would not test the registered method. No baseline or candidate was trained/scored and no storage/quality/runtime metric is claimed.

## C — strongest counter-hypothesis

Any gain may come from native geometry guidance and one-step diffusion itself; ordinary appearance latents or photometric gain could match the proposed code at equal or lower bytes, while Mirror codes may reduce cross-view consistency.

## U — unresolved

Official code/checkpoint/data release, supported hardware, and the Mirror-versus-native frontier remain unconfirmed.
