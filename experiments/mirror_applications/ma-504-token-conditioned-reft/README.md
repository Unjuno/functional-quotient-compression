# MA-504 — token-conditioned Mirror ReFT

Status: FAIL — frozen fresh gates failed
Evidence lane: MECHANISM / STORAGE / RUNTIME
Base commit: `c935a903daca5c7d1d48aa50d05b5bd50f239cba`
Selection: Draw20, uniform over eligible P0/UNTESTED candidates; replay in `source/random_draw.json`.

## H — Hypothesis

A small code generated from each token's context and applied as a Givens rotation inside one shared rank-4 hidden-state intervention can recover context-specific functions at lower actual payload than independent LoReFT interventions, and beat byte-matched conditional FiLM on a held-out mechanism task.

## Mirror insertion

> **Mirror insertion:** this experiment adds a context-generated coordinate `m` between one shared low-rank hidden-state intervention's A and B maps, so that token/context-specific logical functions can be represented without storing an independent intervention per context.

- Physical object: shared rank-4 A/B intervention over 16D hidden features.
- Native method: ReFT/LoReFT hidden-state intervention (PA96).
- Mirror coordinate: six Givens angles generated from a scalar context token.
- Logical multiplicity: two context-specific transforms, evaluated position-wise.
- Simplest comparison: context-generated diagonal FiLM (PA63).
- Additional controls: static LoReFT, two independent context-routed LoReFT modules, independent full-rank intervention upper control.

## T — What ran

The synthetic teacher maps 16D features to 8D outputs through one rank-4 intervention basis with two context-dependent Givens rotations. Two development seeds compared learning rates 0.001 and 0.003 at 700 updates; paired Mirror-minus-FiLM development MSE selected 0.001. Fresh seeds 50403, 50404 and 50405 then ran that rate across static LoReFT, independent routed LoReFT, Mirror, FiLM and a full linear upper control. Each model saw 44,800 training examples. Actual payloads include serialized model tensors and metadata. Audit generation was not invoked.

## Evidence

**Fact.** Mirror MSE on the three fresh seeds was 0.057724, 0.008595 and 0.006742. Independent LoReFT MSE was 0.029270, 0.042633 and 0.041099. Mirror was within 1.10x of independent LoReFT only on seeds 50404/50405; on 50403 it was worse. Mirror payload was 3,428 B versus independent LoReFT's 2,682 B in every seed, failing the ≤80% payload gate. Mirror's payload was byte-matched to FiLM within 0.59% (3,408 B), and Mirror beat FiLM's MSE by at least 2% in all three seeds. Audit remained unopened because not every gate passed.

Mean measured training time was 1.47 s for Mirror, 0.46 s for independent LoReFT, and 0.40 s for FiLM on single-thread CPU. The active multiply-add proxy was 148 per token for Mirror, 96 for independent LoReFT, and 120 for FiLM. Median measured throughput was about 0.21M examples/s for Mirror, 0.71M for independent LoReFT and 1.69M for FiLM. The proxy counts an efficient rank-4 Givens update; runtime reflects the current dense matrix construction in the implementation. Payloads and held-out MSEs for all 35 dev/fresh rows were verified and replayed with maximum difference 0.0.

**Interpretation.** Givens views captured the teacher's context structure more reliably than conditional FiLM at nearly matched payload, but the generator and rotation code made the Mirror model larger and slower than even independent rank-4 LoReFT in this small setting. The experiment therefore does not show compressed representation interventions.

**Hypothesis.** Larger ranks or many more contexts might amortize a shared basis and generator; this screen gives no evidence for that because its fixed two-context payload is already worse and one fresh seed loses quality to the independent control.

## D — Decision

**FAIL.** The payload gate failed in all three fresh seeds, and the independent-quality gate failed in one. The only pass was the byte-matched FiLM quality gate.

## C — Strongest counter-hypothesis

FiLM may express the context-dependent rank modulation more compactly and with the same quality; the Givens generator then adds bytes and compute without Mirror-specific value. Also, a teacher deliberately generated by rotations favors the Mirror family and cannot show natural-token utility.

## U — Unconfirmed

Natural-token language benefit, more than two contexts, private residual savings at scale, convergence, and audit generalization remain untested. Only synthetic mechanism evidence is established; the fixed-update result is not a capacity claim.
