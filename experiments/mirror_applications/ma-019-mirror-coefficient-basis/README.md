# MA-019 — shared expert basis plus Mirror coefficient

Status: **FAIL at development screen**. Fresh worlds remained sealed because Mirror did not show a promising Mirror-specific frontier against a generic rank-2 shared-basis control.
Evidence lane: MECHANISM / STORAGE / RUNTIME.
Branch: `research/ma-019-mirror-coefficient-basis-20261007`.
Base commit: full SHA in `PROTOCOL.json`.

## H — Hypothesis

Eight routed functions generated by unit-circle coefficients over two shared expert matrices can be stored as two matrices plus one angle per role. Generic learned rank-2 coefficients may match it at comparable storage and compute, limiting any Mirror-specific claim. Independent matrices test the basis-capacity boundary.

## T — Test

16D-to-12D linear experts; eight oracle-routed roles. Methods: full MoE, hard tying, shared rank-1 residual, generic learned rank-2 shared basis, and one-angle Mirror basis. Teacher modes: unit-circle combinations over two shared matrices; eight independent matrices. Development world 190000 compared LR 0.003 and 0.01 for 1,200 updates. It selected LR 0.01 by pooled MSE. Fresh worlds were not opened after the development screen exposed a weak Mirror-specific frontier.

## D — Decision: FAIL (development)

### Fact

- On aligned teacher at LR 0.01, Mirror MSE was 5.52e-7 vs full MoE 9.16e-8 and generic rank-2 2.47e-10. Mirror missed the <=1.10x full-MoE quality condition.
- At LR 0.003, Mirror MSE was 0.00622 vs full MoE 0.00710 (within the quality ratio), while generic rank-2 was 8.98e-7. The unconstrained control fit the aligned teacher far more accurately under the same update budget.
- Actual payload: Mirror 3,493B, generic rank-2 3,557B (only 64B or 1.8% more), full MoE 7,913B. Mirror/full was 0.441, missing the 0.40 gate. Mirror and generic rank-2 have the same active compute proxy.
- On independent experts, Mirror MSE was 3.77 and generic rank-2 was 3.61, vs full MoE 8.3e-9 at LR 0.01.
- Twenty development rows replayed; payload bytes matched exactly, max MSE difference 4.7e-10, worst-role difference 5.0e-10, R² difference 4.9e-9. Tests: 3 passed. No fresh worlds opened.

### Interpretation

Two shared bases compress substantially relative to full MoE, but the one-angle code saves only 64 serialized bytes versus generic two-coefficient sharing and learns the aligned basis more slowly. This screen does not show a useful Mirror-specific Pareto improvement; generic shared rank-2 is the stronger control. Independent roles exceed both low-dimensional bases.

### Strongest counter-hypothesis

A per-method LR or longer schedule could reduce Mirror's angle-optimization gap. That would be a separate development amendment and needs fresh seeds; it cannot be inferred from this run. The best observed Mirror result used LR 0.003, but remained far behind generic rank-2.

### U — Unconfirmed

Per-method optimizer tuning, nonlinear experts, learned routing, alternate angle initialization, and near-convergence/fixed-byte frontiers were not tested.

## Fact / interpretation / hypothesis

- **Fact:** rank-2 shared basis compressed vs full MoE; generic coefficients had similar bytes and much lower dev MSE; independent matrices defeated both.
- **Interpretation:** the compact angular address did not improve the frontier over ordinary shared-basis coefficients in this task.
- **Hypothesis:** different coordinate parameterizations may optimize more easily, but require a new preregistered experiment.
