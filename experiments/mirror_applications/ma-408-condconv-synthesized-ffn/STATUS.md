# MA-408 status: PASS in the preregistered synthetic screen

## H — falsifiable hypothesis

Weight synthesis from two FFN basis experts can stay within 1.0pp of executing both experts' output mixture, at no more than 70% of its active MACs and 1.1× its payload bytes.

## T — executed

Four-context synthetic classification whose labels come from context-specific convex mixtures of two fixed FFN teacher logits. Compared shared, CondConv synthesized weights, output-mixture of two experts, rank-one context residual, and independent context FFNs. 200 AdamW updates × batch 128; 2 development worlds × 3 seeds selected learning rate; 3 fresh worlds × 3 seeds. Selected LR: 0.003 shared, 0.01 all others. Context coefficients are shared by each context group; weight-synthesis cost is amortized over the 3,072 training/evaluation records, with all four context weight syntheses included in total MACs. CPU PyTorch 2.14.1.

## D — PASS (bounded mechanism evidence)

Fresh means: CondConv 66.09% accuracy, CE 1.0006, 18,081 bytes, 4,037.25 MAC/example; output-mixture 66.59%, CE 0.9662, 18,081 bytes, 8,064 MAC/example. The 0.50pp accuracy gap is within the 1pp gate; CondConv uses 50.1% of the output-mixture MAC proxy and equal bytes. Mean training wall time was 0.237s CondConv vs 0.115s output-mixture, so this is a MAC/storage result, not a wall-clock win. Shared scored 58.37%; rank-one 61.81%; independent per-context FFNs 62.65% (underfit in this short screen).

## C — strongest counter-hypothesis

The teacher is deliberately generated by output-mixture experts and this controlled context allows amortizing one synthesized matrix across many examples. Per-token changing coefficients or poorly batched deployment can erase the compute advantage. The longer measured training wall time already warns that the MAC proxy does not imply runtime speed.

## U — unresolved

Natural language/Transformer quality, token-wise coefficient changes, learned routing, inference throughput on realistic batches, and near-convergence quality remain untested. No Mirror-specific advantage is claimed; this is the native basis for MA-409.
