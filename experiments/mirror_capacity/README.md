# Mirror functional capacity studies (A1–A12)

**Interpretation as of 2026-11-15:** This exploratory sequence has NOT established a Mirror-specific rate–quality advantage over carefully controlled conditional Dense, FiLM, output-basis, or low-rank models. Distinguish synthetic evidence from actual Transformer compression. Source-code implementations of the models evolve between phases, so do not pool the results as one preregistered trial.

| Study | Purpose | Result and caveat |
|---|---|---|
| A1 (2026-11-14) | Distill 8/16 functions under shear-Mirror, FiLM, independent teacher families; capacity sweep | Matching the teacher's parametric family helps; shear Mirror good on shear teachers, FiLM good on FiLM teachers. Circular teacher matching is not a general Mirror advantage. |
| A2 (2026-11-14) | External-teacher input rotations and ordinary-context MLPs; inverse-removal ablation | Pre-nonlinearity modulation sometimes better than full shear Mirror. Across generic-context MLP teachers, strong controls outperform. |
| A3 (2026-11-14) | Explicit input-rotation adapter and matched-byte controls | Simple input-coordinate adapter dominates on rotation-teacher task; full Mirror not needed. |
| A4/A4b (2026-11-14) | Continual learning of old/new tasks with code-only freeze, replay, rank-matched Mirror4 | Mirrored structure does not reliably beat FiLM / shared output-basis in old/new task Pareto quality, even after increasing code from 1 to 4. A4b is exploratory follow-up. |
| **[A5 (2026-11-15)](A5/README.md)** | Fixed 1500/3000B budget, 6 fresh worlds × 2 seeds × two **non-Mirror** teacher families, six methods | **288 models; Mirror4 loses to dev-selected best non-Mirror control on IID/OOD in 24/24 world × condition comparisons; zero >=10% capacity wins**. Independent teacher quality threshold was unmet by all methods, so some rate comparisons are censored. |
| **[A6 (2026-11-15)](A6/README.md)** | Real handwritten-digit images with 16 known binary functions; 6 distinct mappings/splits of the **same 1,797-image dataset**, two function families, seven student models, 6/12k byte caps | **336 models; Mirror4 wins 0/6 worlds on IID BCE across all four family×budget conditions.** A shared FFN with task-specific output heads wins digit-mask tasks. XOR/ink task is censored at the preregistered quality threshold. No 10% byte saving established. |
| **[A7 (2026-11-15)](A7/README.md)** | 16 compositional image predicates from 8 spatial features (AND/XOR), six new mappings × two seeds, seven matched-weight-cap methods, 6/12k-byte caps | **336 models; Mirror4 has IID and OOD BCE ratio >1 in all 24 mapping×family×cap comparisons, no 10% byte reduction wins.** XOR Mirror minimum passing size remains censored; multihead passed quality in 2/6 mappings. Same underlying digits source, no independent domain replication. |
| **[A8/A8b (2026-11-15)](A8/README.md)** | Rank-certified 16-function hinge/ramp teachers; 6 worlds × 2 seeds × 2 caps × 7 models = 336 trained, plus 32 longer-training models on four fresh worlds | **Exact capacity exists**: shear-Mirror expresses hinge/ramp in 220/244 B from teacher-provided thresholds, but non-Mirror shifts can do the same. **Trained Mirror fails a general saved-byte gate**; ramp 512 B joint-quality pass 0/6 vs dev-selected control 6/6. A8b 2400 updates ramp: Mirror 0/4 vs controls 4/4. Mathematical rank obstruction applies only to shared features + affine task heads; not a proof of Mirror uniqueness. |
| **[A9/A9R (2026-11-15)](A9/README.md)** | Study optimizer reachability, not representational novelty: 16 ramp functions; 216 models/864 scores plus 108 models/432 scores on fresh worlds; compare random init, scaffold, frozen-code, direct shift | **Family-aware fixed scaffold + 32 learned task-code scalars passes joint IID/OOD quality in 5/6 and 6/6 worlds; randomly initialized full Mirror passes 0/6 in both.** Scaffold code-only Mirror, pre-Mirror, and direct shift have the same trained code and function; direct shift full-training passes 6/6 in both. **No Mirror-specific capacity/compression win.** Follow-up selected after A9 is exploratory. |

| **[A10/A10b (2026-11-15)](A10/README.md)** | Learn shared scaffold from 16 labeled synthetic ramp functions; freeze it and fit codes for 8 unseen functions with 32 labeled examples/task. 6 worlds × 2 seeds × 6 methods × 2 exact FP32 caps = 144 models; post-result 4-world 3x-update A10b adds 48 models | **Mirror 0/6 and 2/6 world quality passes at 512/1024B vs FiLM 5/6 and 6/6.** Mirror/FiLM unknown-task IID mean world NRMSE ratios favor FiLM in all 12 matched worlds. 3x update A10b Mirror 2/4→1/4 vs FiLM/Shift 4/4. The 10%-minimum-byte and A10b rescue gates were structurally underidentified; disclose as **UNCERTAIN**, not proofs. Full locked weight evidence in separate ZIP; synthetic single-family study. |
| **[A11 (2026-11-15)](A11/README.md)** | Frozen **288-model** fine-budget comparison across three non-Mirror 1D teacher families (ramp/tent/sine); 2 independently drawn worlds × 2 seeds per family; six methods × byte caps 512/640/768/1024, 16 meta tasks plus eight held-out code-adaptation tasks | **Ramp:** ordinary Shift passes joint seen/IID/OOD NRMSE≤0.20 in both worlds at all four caps, Mirror passes only world 1101 at 764B (vs Shift 500B), no 10% saving. **Tent/sine:** no method passes under any cap; both minimum-byte frontiers are censored, NOT evidence of impossibility. Fixed optimizer step count is not FLOP matching; 2 worlds/family is exploratory only. 288/288 locked checkpoint integrity, 12/12 independent byte-identical replays. |

| **[A12/A12b (2026-11-15)](A12/README.md)** | Separate learned-scaffold meta updates (600/2400), held-out support size (32/256), and code updates (200/800) on tent/sine. 3 new worlds × 2 seeds × Mirror/Shift/FiLM × full factorial = 288 locked models; post-result A12b adds 72 privileged 1024-example code-only/joint-training models. | **Tent:** increasing support improves Mirror unseen-IID error 0.727→0.582 (3/3 worlds); longer meta improves old-task fitting but does not help unseen task fitting. Family-informed regression from just 32 labeled examples can reconstruct all observed tent/sine curves nearly exactly, so pure sample-information scarcity is not sufficient. A12b FiLM joint training passes 3/3 tent worlds vs frozen-code 1/3; Mirror joint passes 0/3. **Sine:** support increase does little, joint training improves IID but OOD fails for all methods. **No Mirror-specific compression win.** A12b is post-discovery exploratory and cannot be pooled as independent preregistration. |

- A1–A4 full synthetic evidence archives are retained in the earlier conversation's downloadable artifacts but are **not** silently reclassified as public Git-hosted checkpoints. The main conclusions above are from those experiment reports; the bundles can be independently introduced under explicit provenance later.
- All recorded model-file sizes exclude common decoder source code and execution frameworks. They are serialized learned weights, **not** a universal description-length lower bound or GPU runtime advantage.
- Selection and additional follow-ups based on earlier observed outcomes mean that cross-study comparisons are exploratory. Never treat increasing experiment count as increasing independent confirmation.

## Next research gate

Prioritize the distinction between **shared feature extraction** and **task-specific readout** after A6–A7's strong multihead results. A8–A9 now separate strict expressibility, parameterization, and reachable training outcomes. A10 tried data-learned scaffold on one ramp family. A11 broadened to ramp/tent/sine and four byte caps; Shift still dominates the quality-passing ramp case, while tent/sine results are right-censored. A12 reveals that tent tasks suffer from frozen-scaffold/code-fitting reachability despite sufficient information in the 32 samples for a family-aware oracle, while sine additionally fails OOD extrapolation. Next test data-driven scaffold discovery across novel function families and explicitly robust periodic-feature controls; equalize measured optimizer work/FLOPs and broaden independent worlds before any general claim. Next evaluate held-out task addresses and real multi-domain tasks, then compare full stored-byte **and matched training/inference compute** frontiers using a frozen protocol. Do not keep increasing shear-Mirror count without a mechanism-specific positive control. If no low-description structured Mirror variant improves on strong controls, favor ordinary shared-basis methods rather than expanding Mirror count.
